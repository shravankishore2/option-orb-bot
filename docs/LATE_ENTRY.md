# Late entries: holding time, P&L share and price realism

Generated by `late_entry_check.py` on 2026-10-07. **Exploratory**: the same walk-forward data as docs/RESULTS.md, so it informs but doesn't decide.

Every walk-forward GO trade of v2 (2,123, 2021-06-01 → 2026-09-22) is re-walked by `exits.simulate_day` on the cached candles: 2,123 reproduce the stored P&L exactly (0 missing candles, 0 mismatches). Tables use the combined period 2022-01-01 → 2026-09-22 (1,863 trades), the same as docs/THRESHOLD_RESULTS.md and docs/V3_RESULTS.md, unless they say otherwise.

**How a trade is timed:** a candle stamped T covers T..T+5. A signal from it is entered at T+5 at that candle's close, and the exit walk starts with the candle stamped T+5. The last entry is 15:10 (from the 15:05 candle). The forced exit is the close of the first candle stamped 15:15 or later, normally 15:20.

## Summary

- **Holding time:** median 30 minutes over all GO trades. Entries at or after 15:00 are held 15 minutes (median), at most 20.
- **Late entries are 42% of the trades but 19% of the gross P&L** (8% after 0.05%). They average +0.071% per trade gross, +0.021% after 0.05% and -0.029% after 0.10%: about break-even after costs, though they win more often (54% vs 52%). The label rewards winning, not size, which is a plausible reason the model rates these short trades highly.
- **Without them** v2's mean per trade is +0.228% instead of +0.162% (total after 0.10%: +137.4 vs +114.6). This is a post-hoc cut on design-period data, so it is a hypothesis for a new protocol, not a result.
- **Prices are tradable:** the next candle's open is on average the same as the signal candle's close, so filling at the next open changes nothing. Every forced exit uses the 15:15 candle, closing at 15:20, ten minutes before the 15:30 close and outside any closing-auction price. The exit candle trades well above a normal candle's volume.
