## Reading and recommendation

Written by hand after the run; the tables above are generated.

**1. Big winners.** P&L is extremely concentrated:
- The top 1% of all signals (gains of +3.8% or more) earn as much as the whole set does in total, 95% of it. The top 10% earn four times the total, because the rest net out negative.
- v2 doesn't pick these winners more often than chance. Among its GO trades there are as many top-1% and top-5% winners as random picks of the same size, and fewer top-10% winners (187 against 217 [189, 245]).
- Its edge over random (+0.168% against +0.066% per trade, gross) comes from elsewhere. It takes slightly fewer big losers (182 of its trades are in the bottom 10%, against about 212 expected at random).
- Its picks lean late in the day (docs/LATE_ENTRY.md), and late trades have little time to become big winners.

**2. Market alignment** doesn't make the signals profitable after costs:
- **Aligned with NIFTY:** −0.038% per trade after 0.10% costs, against −0.055% for trades going against it.
- **The rule** (aligned and breadth agrees): −0.028%, with 46 signals a day. It's positive after 0.10% in only one year of six, 2024.
- **Breadth:** the gradient is monotonic (−0.173% when under 30% of stocks move with the trade, −0.024% when over 70% do), but no bucket clears 0.10%.
- **NIFTY flips:** its direction at the signal flips by the close for 24% of signals. That's 32% for signals before 10:00 and 10% after 14:15, one more reason late signals look "safer" to a model trained on the profit label.

**3–6. New labels.** None of the 24 candidate/rate combinations beats v2 under the pre-set rule. That's before any correction for testing 24 of them, which would only make the bar higher.
- **Closest:** (a) P&L > 0.10% with 25 inputs. Its mean-per-trade difference from v2 is positive at every rate: +0.015%, +0.016%, +0.018% and +0.010% at 2%, 5%, 10% and 20%. But every CI includes zero.
- **Totals:** it has a higher total after 0.10% at every rate (+170 against +145 at 2%; +434 against +246 at 10%).
- **It depends less on June 2024:**
  - v2's best month is 30% of its gross P&L at 2%, against 16% for this candidate;
  - excluding June 2024, v2 at 2% totals +44 after 0.10%, against +114 for this candidate and +117 for the same label with 28 inputs.
- **The weighted classifier** (c) is worse than v2 at every rate.
- **The regression** (b) matches v2 with 28 inputs and is worse with 25. Plain squared error on returns with ±10% tails is a likely reason.

**Recommendation:**
- **Don't promote anything.** No candidate passed, so per the brief there is no draft shadow-variant protocol.
- **v2 stays as it is.** The cost-aware classifier, (a) P&L > 0.10% with 25 inputs, is the one direction worth a properly pre-registered test after the late-October decision. Its edge over v2 is small and not significant, but consistent in sign at every rate. It is less dependent on one month, and it doesn't reward small late wins the way the profit label does.
- **Any such test would need its own protocol:** the candidate and rate fixed in advance, a test on the late slice like docs/LATE_CUT_PROTOCOL.md, and live data only, since all of this history is design-period data.
- **Market alignment** is already in the model's inputs. As a stand-alone rule it adds nothing after costs, so I wouldn't pursue it separately.
