What didn't work

Where the results fell short

Model v1 failed its pre-registered test

It predicted its training label well out of sample (AUC 0.64–0.71), but higher scores meant less profit: bottom score decile +0.145% a trade, top decile +0.010%. It favoured narrow-range setups that hit "+1.5× range" easily but made little money. Most profit actually comes from trades that drift for hours and exit at 15:15, which the label ignored.

Model v2's edge is concentrated and partly an artefact

Feb 2022 (the Ukraine crash) is 61% of its 2022–23 profit; without it v2 equals the unfiltered pool (+0.052% vs +0.052%, p = 0.30). June 2024 (election result) is 48% of its development profit, though without it v2 still beats random picks (p = 0.025). 34–46% of its trades enter after 15:00 and last 5–15 minutes, which costs would eat. Deflated Sharpe 0.01 after ~200 variants tried.

Costs erase the rules' edge

At a realistic 0.05–0.10% round trip, the rules' +0.054% gross becomes zero or negative. Costs are shown as a sensitivity line only, since you asked to add them later.

Still open

Known limitations, not fixed

Research

  • v2 needs ≥150 live GO trades (~4–5 months) to pass or fail its forward test.
  • v3 should stop entries ~14:30 so every trade has time to work. It needs a new pre-registration.
  • Universe proxy is 83% accurate; merged/delisted companies (e.g. HDFC Ltd) aren't in Dhan at all.
  • No cost or slippage model; option P&L not modelled.

Operations

  • Dhan tokens expire daily; the bot alerts and stops on 401.
  • Dhan's recent sessions lack their 15:15–15:25 candles, so recent trades exit at the 15:10 close.
  • Each live cycle takes 50–70 s (~200 requests at the rate limit).
  • The bot sends entries only; it doesn't manage exits or place orders.
  • CSV files as storage (kept, as you asked).
