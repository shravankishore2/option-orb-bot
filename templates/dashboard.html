About ORBITAL

ORBITAL scans the Nifty 200 for opening-range breakouts, then asks a gradient-boosted model whether each breakout looks like one that follows through. Only the ones it rates in the top couple of percent are sent. Everything — live bot and backtest — runs the same code, so what you see here is what was tested.

1 · Signal

After the 09:20–09:35 range forms, each completed 5-minute candle is checked: a break of the range, a ±1.8% move from yesterday's close, and a pivot confirmation.

2 · Score

28 inputs — range geometry, volume, VWAP, volatility, sector and index context — go into XGBoost, retrained monthly. It predicts whether the trade will make money under the exact exit rule used below.

3 · Act

GO signals are sent to Telegram with a stop and trail. Everything else is logged with its score, so the model's choices can be audited later.
